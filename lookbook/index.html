What makes it Gazelle

Every piece is cut on the CNC from 18 mm Baltic birch and laminated in pairs to 36 mm. Each ply is rounded over 5/8" on its outer face, so every edge shows its laminations as stripes that follow the outline. These details come from Titobi’s shop files and shop photos.

The collection

Seven pieces come straight from Titobi’s shop files; the rest are built from those files’ parts and construction. The dining table was designed in Vectary for the set. Every piece comes in Baltic birch and four hardwoods. Hover a piece for its drawing.

Drawings

Line drawings come straight from the models, in the style of Titobi’s own typology drawings: outlines and part breaks, no shading.

Exploded

Every piece pulled apart along its assembly axes.

In rooms

Rendered in Vectary from the final models: the furniture is the exact 3D render, and Vectary made the room, light and styling around it.

Language of Style

Gazelle seating is a whole family of styles. The side chair, armchair, bullhorn chair, counter stools, lounge chair, rocker and settee all speak one language of laminated plies and long, bent members, so there is a Gazelle for any setting: the dining table, the kitchen counter, the desk, the living room and the front hall.

Materials

Baltic birch shows its plies on every edge. Walnut, white oak, wenge and maple are solid hardwood, with the grain running along each member. The textures were made in Vectary.
